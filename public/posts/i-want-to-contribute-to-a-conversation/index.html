I Want to Contribute to a Conversation

November 22, 2025

I want to contribute to a conversation

I’ve been trying to understand what I really want from my career, music, and how i spend my time in general. I love to find a nice coffee shop or restaurant around the east village and write about my thoughts. Thats how I’ve spent a good portion of my free weekends and mornings and evenings since moving to NYC but also for long before then. Doing work and talking about it with friends and other people is such a core part of what i enjoy most in life. Work isn’t necessarily work-work, but just anything that takes some effort to get an interesting outcome. A night at a cocktail bar or a birthday party or a home cooked dinner or ordering furniture are all work that bring joy to my life, and then talking about those things with friends or experiencing them with friends or with co-workers or strangers and understanding what about that work was meaningful and what was funny and what you want to do next is so PEAK life to me.

In high school and college, life was abound with collaborative work like that. I’m finding ways to do it in NYC as well. Hosting dinner parties out here is one of my favorite ways of doing that. Working on music is another way I’m trying to get involved in that too. The minor, 1-book, 3person book club we ran in the summer with Amanda and Colin was another time that my life felt more full because of work and collaboration. It only feels rewarding when you actually put in the time and effort into it. Being able to discuss each week and read together and sit around the table with snacks and reflect on our personal lives together is a product of taking out the time and energy to do something properly all the way through. Or maybe 80% of the way through. I think its gotta be at least 60%. There’s a threshold where it detracts from the experience to do it below that. Like if you’re only 30% there all the time it feels more negative for the spirit compared to not doing anything at all and evaluating what you actually have the time and energy for. Thats a bit of a digression though. The music video filming and releasing this year was another example of something i put time into and collaborated on.

Going into the end of this year and the start of 2026 I want to keep those types of activities on the forefront of my planning.
