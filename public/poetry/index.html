At the Brink

June 01, 2025

Feeling pretty comfortable till now I’m at the brink
Guess I’m not as put together as I think
I keep on growing & showing myself theres more to see
But you don’t think of that when you go crashing down hopelessly

I feel far away from where I’m supposed to be

Other Than the End

May 01, 2025

​ We met to take a walk before you left
​ Was just so glad we’d get to talk again
​ And something on your patient faded breath
​ Had made it even harder to pretend

​ Today was any other than the end
​ The base our careful balance on could should shift
​ Soon as we face each other but instead
​ We fix our gaze at waves beyond the cliff

Blossoms

March 30, 2025

Couldn’t gather patience in the garden
While they posed for pictures with the blossoms
Until I remembered how important
It was catching my own fleeting moments

When you feel the daylight dying colder
Walking with your love and never knowing
If there is another chance for photos
Blossoms by the winter will be frozen
