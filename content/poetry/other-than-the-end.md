​	We met to take a walk before you left  
​	Was just so glad we'd get to talk again  
​	And something on your patient faded breath  
​	Had made it even harder to pretend

​	Today was any other than the end  
​	The base our careful balance on could should shift  
​	Soon as we face each other but instead  
​	We fix our gaze at waves beyond the cliff  

​	And in a couple hours all the morning fog will lift



***



Notes:

- Spenserian stanza writing exercise *abab bcbc c* with iambic pentameter lines for the first eight and an alexandrine (six-foot line) for the final
