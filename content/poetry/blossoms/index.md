Couldn't gather patience in the garden  
While they posed for pictures with the blossoms  
Until I remembered how important  
It was catching my own fleeting moments  

When you feel the daylight dying colder  
Walking with your love and never knowing  
If there is another chance for photos  
Blossoms by the winter will be frozen
