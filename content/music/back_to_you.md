Back To You is a song I wrote when i was questioning:

- how much of who i am is dependent on the people who love me?
- when i leave them or they leave me, how much of me is still there?
- what happens when i see them again? will they still see me for who i want to be?

Very melodramatic!



The vision for the song is very grand and melancholic.

Things I like in the demo that contribute to the grand melancholy vibe:
- The chorus lyrics and melody
- The lead trumpet melody
- The drum fills that transition between sections
- The space left between the chorus and first verse, and first verse and second chorus
- The bassline that pushes it along nicely

I played this with a band at an open mic on March 9th, and their rendition added a lot to the song. It made me feel very inspired to enhance my production in the demo. I think it could benefit from a lot of the ideas in the live performance:
- The drums! I love the drummer
- The dreamy electric keyboard melodies
- The urgency. The live band did a great job of building up to big drops throughout the song, especially the lengthy second verse.
