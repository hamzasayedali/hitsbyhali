> "he convinced himself, as we all do, that one person or one thing is going to change everything"

This is a quote from a podcast about Scott Pilgrim vs The World. This weekend I've been in the mood where every little phrase and song has the chance to ring true with me but this in particular has resonant.

I feel this way because often in my last couple years of struggling with life's purpose, I keep running up against the idea that there is a single thing I need to do to fix things. I need to make my album or get a new job. 

Yearning -- I'm conflicted about this emotion. I've historically been a big yearner. But I think yearning without understanding that whatever you yearn for might not be what you expect and definitely will not change *everything* is so important.
