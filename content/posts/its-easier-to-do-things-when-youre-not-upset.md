It's so uninteresting to me to pull up to work being upset about being there every day and moping about the time i have to spend there and being stressed about not having things done.



Firstly, you need to be comfortable with things undone as long as you work towards getting them done. Thats the whole point of having a job. There are undone things that someone needs to do. Firstly, removing the stress and fear about that regular part of the job is big.



Secondly, being upset and procrastinating because my dayjob isn't what I want to be doing full time does not make me any closer at being able to do something else. I'm glad I have the signal that I want to move on to something else, but what have I done with that signal actually? You need to channel the energy into actual real work, and one of the best things I can do to achieve that is to work well at my current job because that experience is the only thing making it easier to find new jobs.

Lastly, I just don't like to be upset. It feels like it detracts from who I want to be as Hamza. I rely on myself and people rely on me to be positive and optimistic about life. It's one of the core tenets of how I think about myself. I want to carry it forward more strongly.
